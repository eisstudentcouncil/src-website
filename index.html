Why we exist

Three things we're actually responsible for

Not a resume line; this is the job. Every executive and rep is accountable to one of these.

Represent

Carry your complaints to the people who can fix them

Class reps collect concerns every homeroom and bring them to the principal's cabinet meeting each month; with names attached, not vague vibes.

Organize

Run the events that make this school feel like a place

Homecoming, spirit week, the talent show, blood drives; council handles the permits, the vendors, and the cleanup crew.

Fund

Raise money and spend it where students voted for it

Every dollar from bake sales and dances goes into a public ledger, and the student body votes on where the surplus goes each spring.

Get involved

You don't need to be elected to help

Most of what council does is committee work; setup crews, decorating, fundraising, outreach. Here's how to get on a list.

01

Sign up to be a volunteer

Enter this form and we'll add you to the student council volunteer list.

02

Approval

Once approved you'll get a weekly email with upcoming opportunities and occasional committee meetings.

03

Committee Work

Sign up for the events and initiatives that interest you; no minimum commitment, just show up when you can.

Drop something in the suggestion box

Read at every Wednesday meeting. Leave your name if you want a reply; anonymous is fine too.
